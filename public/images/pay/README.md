Payment logos shown at checkout. Put the official files here (PNG or SVG,
transparent background, about 64 x 40 px or larger):

  airtel.png  (or .svg)   Airtel Money
  mtn.png                 MTN MoMo
  zamtel.png              Zamtel Kwacha
  visa.png                Visa
  mastercard.png          Mastercard

Until a file is here, checkout shows the name as text instead.
Use the brands' official merchant / acceptance logos (Visa and Mastercard
brand centres, or ask Lipila for their payment-method logo pack).

visa.svg, mastercard.svg and airtel.svg come from Simple Icons
(https://simpleicons.org, CC0), coloured in each brand's colour.
mtn.svg and zamtel.svg are simple stand-ins in the brands' colours: replace
them with the official files when you have them (same names). A group of
logos (the networks, or Visa and Mastercard) only shows once every logo in
it has loaded; until then the names show as text.
