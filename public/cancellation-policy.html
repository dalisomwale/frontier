Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how orders placed on Frontier Marketplace (marketplace.frontierfc.co.zm), operated by Frontier Farms & Consult Ltd, can be cancelled by you or by us. It applies to livestock and animal feed orders.

1. How orders work

When you place an order, the animals or feed you choose are reserved for you. Our team or the seller confirms the order, you complete payment, and we contact you to arrange collection. An order is complete once the animals or feed have been handed over to you or your representative.

2. Cancelling your order

You may cancel an order at any time before handover, free of charge:

  • Before payment: the order is cancelled and nothing is owed.
  • After payment, before handover: the order is cancelled and you receive a full refund, as set out in our Refund Policy.

Once the animals or feed have been handed over and accepted, the order can no longer be cancelled. Any problem after handover is handled under our Return Policy.

3. How to cancel

To cancel, contact us with your order number (for example FM-1042) by replying to your order email, emailing info@frontierfc.co.zm, or calling us. We confirm every cancellation by email.

4. Cancellation by Frontier

We may cancel an order if:

  • the animals or feed are no longer available, or the seller cannot fulfil the order;
  • the listing contained a clear error, such as a wrong price;
  • payment is not completed within 3 days of the order being confirmed;
  • we reasonably suspect fraud or misuse of the Platform.

If we cancel an order you have already paid for, you receive a full refund. We will always tell you by email when we cancel an order, and why.
