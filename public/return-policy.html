Return Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Live animals and animal feed need special care, so returns work differently from other goods. This policy explains what can be returned to Frontier Farms & Consult Ltd after an order from Frontier Marketplace (marketplace.frontierfc.co.zm) has been handed over.

1. Inspect before you accept

All orders are collected, and you or your representative can inspect the animals or feed before accepting them. This is the best time to raise a problem: anything that does not match your order can be declined on the spot for a full refund, as set out in our Refund Policy. We strongly recommend checking the number, breed, sex, age and condition of animals, and the product, quantity, packaging and expiry date of feed, before accepting.

2. Livestock

To protect animal health and prevent the spread of disease, live animals cannot be returned for a change of mind once they have been accepted and moved. We accept a livestock return only if, within 48 hours of handover:

  • the animals delivered do not match what was ordered (for example the wrong number, breed or sex) and this could not reasonably be seen at handover; or
  • a registered veterinarian confirms in writing that an animal had an illness or condition before handover that could not reasonably be seen at inspection.

Returned animals must be kept separately and cared for properly until they are collected, and must be in the same condition as when they were handed over, apart from the reported problem.

3. Animal feed

  • Unopened bags can be returned within 7 days of handover if they are the wrong product, damaged or past their expiry date.
  • Opened bags can be returned only if the feed is defective (for example mouldy, contaminated or infested) and you tell us within 48 hours of handover, with photos.

Feed must be stored in a dry, clean place until it is returned.

4. How to return

Contact us with your order number, a description of the problem and photos or a veterinary report. We review every request within one working day and, if it is approved, arrange collection of the animals or feed. Once a return is received and checked, we either replace the items or refund you, as you prefer, under our Refund Policy.

5. Return transport

If the return is due to our error or the seller's, we cover the cost of collecting the animals or feed. Otherwise any transport cost is agreed with you before the return.
