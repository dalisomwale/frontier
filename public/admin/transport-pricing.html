Transport pricing & settings

What buyers pay for transport, by road distance. Providers never set prices. A delivery keeps the price it was booked at, even if the bands change later.

Distance price bands

A journey of d km uses the active band where from ≤ d < to (no to means no upper limit). Fee = base fee + price per km × d.

Sellers' pickup points

Buyers can only choose transport for a seller's listings once the seller has a pickup point. Sellers set it in their profile; you can set or correct it here.
