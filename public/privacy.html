Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

1. Who we are

Frontier Marketplace (“the Platform”, “we”, “us”) is operated by Frontier Farms & Consult Ltd. It lets visitors browse livestock listings published by our team and send us an inquiry about a listing. Visitors do not need to create an account.

2. What we collect

When you send an inquiry, we collect:

  • Inquiry details: your full name, phone number, email address and the message you write.
  • The listing you asked about: its title, category and breed, attached automatically.
  • Technical data: your IP address and the date and time of the inquiry, used to prevent spam and abuse.

If you only browse the Platform, we do not collect any personal information about you.

3. How we use it

We use inquiry information only to:

  • Contact you about the livestock you inquired about, by phone or email.
  • Keep a record of inquiries so our team can follow up and track their progress.
  • Detect and prevent spam, fraud and abuse of the inquiry form.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Who sees it

We do not sell your personal information, and inquiries are never shown publicly. Your inquiry is visible only to the Frontier Marketplace team: it is stored in our database and sent by email to the team members who handle inquiries. We share data with service providers who help us run the Platform (hosting and email delivery) under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond to you and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping. Our team may archive or delete inquiries once they are resolved.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us in an inquiry, or object to how we use it. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority. To make a request, contact us using the details in section 9.

7. Security

The Platform uses HTTPS in production. Inquiry data can only be viewed by our administrators, whose accounts are protected by securely hashed passwords and server-side sessions. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Cookies

Visitors browsing the Platform receive no cookies. A single session cookie is used only to keep our administrators signed in to the admin dashboard. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Homepage photographs are loaded from Unsplash’s image service, which receives standard request information (such as your IP address) when serving them.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Send an inquiry through the Platform, or contact Frontier Farms & Consult Ltd using its published contact details.
