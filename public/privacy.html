Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Frontier Farms & Consult Ltd (“Frontier”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information on Frontier Marketplace (marketplace.frontierfc.co.zm).

1. What we collect

  • Inquiries: your name, phone number, email address and message, and the listing you asked about.
  • Orders: your name, phone number, email address, the items ordered and any notes you add.
  • Payments: the payment method, the mobile money number used, the payment status and reference. For card payments we keep only the card type (for example Visa) and the last four digits. We never receive or store full card numbers, expiry dates, CVV codes or mobile money PINs; these go directly to our payment provider.
  • Sellers: name, farm or business name, phone, email, province, location, a securely hashed password, and the listings and photos you add.
  • Technical data: IP address and the date and time of requests, used for security and to prevent abuse.

If you only browse the Platform, we do not collect personal information about you.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries and process and confirm orders, arrange collection and support you afterwards.
  • To take payments and to issue refunds.
  • To send emails about your inquiries, orders, payments and seller account.
  • To review seller accounts and listings.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as needed with:

  • Payment service providers, to process payments and refunds;
  • Sellers, who receive order details needed to prepare your order but not your contact details;
  • Service providers who host the Platform and deliver our emails, under confidentiality obligations;
  • authorities, where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as required by tax and accounting law. Inquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and follow up. Seller accounts are kept while they are active, and you can ask us to close yours.

5. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2021, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

6. Security

The Platform uses HTTPS throughout. Passwords are stored as secure hashes, and admin and seller areas use protected server-side sessions. Card payments are handled by our payment provider, so card details never reach our servers.

7. Cookies

Buyers browsing the Platform receive no cookies. Session cookies are used only to keep sellers and our team signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Some photos on the homepage are loaded from Unsplash’s image service, which receives standard request information (such as your IP address) when serving them.
