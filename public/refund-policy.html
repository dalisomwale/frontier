Refund Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains when and how Frontier Farms & Consult Ltd refunds payments made for orders on Frontier Marketplace (marketplace.frontierfc.co.zm).

1. When you get a full refund

You receive a full refund of the amount you paid when:

  • you cancel a paid order before handover (see our Cancellation Policy);
  • we cancel your order, for example because the animals or feed are no longer available;
  • at collection, the animals do not match the order or listing (for example a different number, breed, sex or age), or are visibly sick, injured or dead, and you decline them;
  • at collection, the feed is the wrong product, damaged, contaminated or past its expiry date, and you decline it;
  • a return is approved under our Return Policy;
  • you were charged more than once for the same order.

2. Partial refunds

If only part of an order can be supplied, or you accept part of an order and decline the rest for one of the reasons above, we refund the price of the part not supplied.

3. When refunds are not given

Refunds are not given:

  • after you or your representative have inspected and accepted the animals or feed at handover, except as allowed by our Return Policy;
  • for a change of mind after handover;
  • for illness, injury or death of animals after handover that results from transport, housing, feeding or care after handover;
  • for transport, veterinary or other costs you arranged yourself, unless we agreed to cover them in writing.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid to the original payment method:

  • Mobile money (Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, Zamtel Kwacha): to the number used for payment, within 7 working days of the refund being approved.
  • Visa or Mastercard: to the card used for payment. We start the refund within 7 working days of approval; your bank may take a further 5 to 10 working days to show it.

We refund the full amount paid for the cancelled or declined items, with no deductions. If the original method cannot receive the refund, we will agree another method with you and confirm it in writing.

5. How to request a refund

Most refunds start automatically when an order is cancelled. For anything else, contact us within 48 hours of collection with your order number, a description of the problem and, where possible, photos or a veterinary report. We reply within one working day.
